## Description
ClarityVideo is an on-device video enhancement app built for footage that looks soft, noisy, compressed, or dated.

Import a video from Photos, Files, or Camera, choose an enhancement preset, compare a real processed preview, then export an enhanced copy without replacing your original.

Features in the public App Store build include:

• On-device video enhancement using supported Apple media and machine-learning technology  
• Balanced, Quality, and Ultra enhancement presets  
• Adjustable denoise, detail recovery, and sharpening  
• Before-and-after preview  
• 4K export  
• Up to 8K export on devices that pass ClarityVideo’s hardware capability checks  
• HEVC and supported H.264 export options  
• Photos, Files, Camera, and system Share Sheet workflows  
• Pause/resume checkpoints for long jobs  
• Storage and thermal safeguards  
• No account required  
• No analytics or tracking  
• No cloud upload by ClarityVideo

Processing speed, 8K availability, and supported enhancement routes depend on the source video and the capabilities reported by the device.

## Review notes
ClarityVideo processes selected videos locally on the device and does not require an account.

To review the primary path:
1. Open ClarityVideo.
2. Choose **Enhance Video**.
3. Import a short video from Photos or Files.
4. Leave **4K** and **Apple SR** selected.
5. Use the before/after preview if the device reports Apple Super Resolution support.
6. Tap **Continue**, then **Start Export**.
7. Save or share the completed output.

8K controls are shown only when the device passes ClarityVideo’s real hardware encoder capability probe. Camera and microphone permissions are requested only when Camera import is selected. Long on-device processing may take time and can pause when the device reaches a critical thermal state.

The public App Store build does not include the separate experimental recovered neural-model build.
