# ClarityVideo Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 22, 2026

ClarityVideo is designed to process video on your device.

## Data collection

ClarityVideo does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit personal data to the developer or to advertising or analytics services. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

## Video and media access

ClarityVideo can access videos you explicitly select from Photos or Files, and it can use the camera and microphone when you choose to record a new video. Selected or recorded media is copied into the app's local workspace so it can be enhanced and exported.

Your source and enhanced videos remain on your device unless you explicitly save, export, or share them using system features such as Photos, Files, or the iOS share sheet.

## On-device processing

Video enhancement, preview generation, diagnostics, and export are performed on-device using Apple media, graphics, and machine-learning technologies and the app's bundled processing components.

## Local app data

ClarityVideo stores local preferences, capability results, recent job information, checkpoints, and temporary processing files needed to provide its features. Temporary processing data can be cleared from the app.

## Tracking

ClarityVideo does not track you across apps or websites.

## Network access

ClarityVideo's app code does not upload your videos or send analytics data to a server.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when ClarityVideo's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, use the Issues section of the ClarityVideo GitHub repository.
